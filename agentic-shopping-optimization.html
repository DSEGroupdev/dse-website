The Shift

Shopping changed. Your storefront did not.

For twenty years, every optimization was for human eyes: prettier pages, faster loads, better photos. That era just ended. The shopper is increasingly software, an agent acting for a human, comparing five stores in seconds and buying from the one it can understand. If your product data, promotions, and checkout only make sense to a person scrolling, you are not in the comparison at all.

What We Found

We let buying agents loose on real checkouts.

We did not run a survey. We pointed real AI buying agents at real transactions. Discovery worked. Checkout did not. Every failure below happened at the money moment, and every one of them is happening on stores like yours right now.

Failure

Your Catalog Lies To Agents

A product feed listed an item at one price. The live store charged more than double. An agent acting on stale feed data misquotes your price, or abandons you for a competitor whose data is clean.

Failure

Your Discounts Are Rumors

A promo code advertised at one discount applied at a lower one. With no machine-readable promotion feed, the agent hunted coupon codes like a human with fourteen tabs open. If your offers cannot be read by software, they do not exist to software.

Failure

Your Variants Need A Translator

One color name meant three different colorways. The agent had to interpret which one the shopper meant, and that is judgment, not data. Every ambiguous variant is a wrong item shipped or a sale lost.

Failure

Your Totals Arrive Too Late

Shipping and tax were unknowable without a full address, so the agent could not show a real total for approval. Nobody, human or agent, approves a number they cannot see.

Failure

Your Checkout Speaks Human Only

With no agent checkout interface enabled, the agent drove the website like a person: slow, brittle, one bot check from dead. That is not a channel. That is a hack.

Failure

Your Payments Reject Agents

The agent did everything right, found the bill, verified the amount, secured explicit human approval, and the processor declined the agent-issued card. When agents cannot pay you, they pay someone else.

Questions

Asked before nearly every build.

Is Shopify already handling this?

Shopify provides the rails; we make you win on them. Being technically purchasable is not the same as being chosen, quoted correctly, and checked out cleanly. Our tests prove the gap: stale feeds, unreadable promotions, and human-only checkouts exist on Shopify stores today.

How is a selling agent different from a chatbot?

A chatbot answers questions on your site. A selling agent closes sales across every channel, conversing with human shoppers on your storefront and negotiating with their buying agents in the background, inside pricing and policy guardrails you define.

Are buying agents really shopping yet?

Yes. AI platforms already transact through agentic checkout on major retailers, and every platform is expanding it. The question is not whether agents will shop your category. It is whether your store will be readable when they arrive.

Do we have to be on Shopify?

No. We work with Shopify, custom platforms, and multi-brand catalogs. The standards we build on are platform-agnostic, and the readiness sprint will tell you exactly what your stack needs.

Does the agent replace our website?

No. Your site stays the home for human shoppers. The agent is an additional salesperson on it, and simultaneously a machine-readable presence for the agents shopping in the background.

How does this relate to an AI sales agent?

They are separate offerings that share DNA. An AI sales agent sells to the humans visiting your store. Agentic Shopping Optimization makes your entire store legible and transactable to the software shopping on their behalf, then puts a selling agent in both conversations. Each works fully on its own.
